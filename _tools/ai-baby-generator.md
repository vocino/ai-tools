AI Baby Generator is a browser-based tool for creating imagined baby or child portrait concepts from two adult photographs, with age, gender and resemblance options. It can supply a fictional character reference for a creative project, but its images are not genetic, medical or parentage predictions. Photo generation currently requires 40 credits; a separate illustrative eye-color calculator is free.
