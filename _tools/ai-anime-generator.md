AI Anime Generator is a browser-based image tool for developing original anime character, portrait, and scene concepts. It accepts text prompts and optional reference images, with selectable visual styles, image shapes, and resolutions. Creators can use it to explore illustrations for stories, avatars, and design briefs, then review and download candidate images. The service uses paid credit packs or subscriptions; usage terms and rights for references should be checked before publishing generated material.
