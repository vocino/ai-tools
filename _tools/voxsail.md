VoxSail is a browser-based workspace for transcribing uploaded video or audio, translating subtitles, and generating AI dubbing. Creators and teams can review text and timing in an online editor, then export localized media, subtitle files, and transcripts according to the project workflow. An account and uploaded media are required; paid subscriptions and credit add-ons support ongoing use, while new accounts receive one-time starter credits rather than a renewing free allowance.
