Digital Love Letter is a browser-based personal letter workspace for writing your own text or reviewing an optional AI draft. Writers can add photos and supported music links, then inspect an opening-envelope preview before delivery. Drafting and previews are free, while recipient sharing uses credits; creators should review the wording and keep their editing links private.
