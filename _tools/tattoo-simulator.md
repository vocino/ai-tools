Tattoo Simulator helps users compare a separate artwork image on a body photo as a placement reference. Local positioning and PNG export do not use AI or generation credits; an optional paid AI feature generates cover concepts. A visual mockup is not a prediction of a finished tattoo or healing, and the author of this contribution is associated with the project.
