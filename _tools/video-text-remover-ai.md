Video Text Remover AI is a browser-based cleanup tool for removing burned-in text and captions from short videos you own or have permission to edit. Mark up to three fixed rectangular regions with individual time ranges, then submit the clip for server-side AI processing; regions do not track moving text, and detailed or moving backgrounds may leave artifacts. Compatible MP4, MOV and WebM clips are limited to 50 MB and 60 seconds, subject to browser codec support. Paid credit packs start at USD 4.90 for 100 credits, with subscription plans and up to two conditional trial runs for eligible clips up to 15 seconds, subject to verification, shared usage limits and availability; review the full output before publishing.
