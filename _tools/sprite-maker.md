Sprite Maker is a browser-based AI sprite generator for exploring character concepts, direction views and NPC sets. It includes local pixel editing and transparent PNG export so users can refine and inspect candidate images before a separate production review. Local editing and export are free, while AI generation requires sign-in and credits and may involve paid options.
