MusiCraft AI is an all-in-one AI music agent with a chat-based workspace. It can generate full songs from a prompt, a hum, or a photo, remix and extend tracks, and split a mix into stems. It also cleans and masters audio, then transcribes recordings into sheet music, MIDI, or MusicXML, or scans printed scores from a photo or PDF.
