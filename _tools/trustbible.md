TrustBible answers personal and theological Bible questions with passages readers can check directly. It combines exact KJV or Chinese Union Version citations with pastoral reflections, prayer prompts, topic discovery, and original Bible studies. Ten questions per day are available without an account, and the site supports English and Simplified Chinese readers.
