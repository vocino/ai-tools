CodeI Lab is a third-party gateway that sells GPT API credit at a fraction of official pay-as-you-go prices, aimed at coding agents rather than chat apps. You point Claude Code, Codex CLI or OpenCode at it with one command, using either OpenAI or Claude protocols, and then call GPT-6 Astra/Sol, GPT-6.1 Sol/Terra or GPT-5.6 Sol/Terra through the same credit balance.

Pricing is paid usage-based with no free plan: Trial at $3 per week, Plus at $9.90 per month, Pro at $39.90 per month, and Ultra at $99.90 per month. The service is independent and not affiliated with OpenAI, and only GPT models are served.
