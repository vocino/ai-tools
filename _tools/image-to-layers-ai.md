Image to Layers AI separates flat artwork into pieces that can be moved or edited independently, which can help when preparing a design handoff without the original working file. The browser studio lets you review and adjust the stack before exporting transparent PNGs or a layered PSD. Built-in examples are available without an account; processing your own images requires credits. Background filling estimates hidden pixels rather than recovering the original artwork.
