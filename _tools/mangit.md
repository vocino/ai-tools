Mangit is an AI manga translator for iPhone and Android. You pick a screenshot or take a photo of a page, and it detects the speech bubbles, reads the text, removes the original lettering and writes the translation back into the same bubble, so the page reads like a translated edition rather than a page covered in overlay boxes. New users get 3 free pages, then 3 free pages every week, with paid plans for more.
