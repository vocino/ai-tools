Genogram Maker is a browser-based editor for mapping people and family relationships across generations. Users can build a diagram manually or start with an optional AI draft, then review connections and adjust the layout. The free plan shown includes two projects, up to 50 people per project, and PNG export, while AI use is credit-based after sign-in. Use fictional or appropriately consented information, and check uncertain details before sharing a chart.
