Yeri AI is a browser-based creative studio that puts leading image and video models in one workspace. Generate stills from text or reference images with Seedream, Nano Banana and GPT Image, then turn them into motion with Kling and Seedance. Built-in utilities cover background removal, AI upscaling, photo effects and templates, and an editor lets you refine results before export. A free tier is available so makers and marketers can test the workflow before upgrading.
