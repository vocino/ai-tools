Screenpipe captures screen text and audio locally to create searchable work history for recall, meeting notes, and work summaries. Its [MCP server](https://docs.screenpipe.com/mcp-server) and local API let compatible AI assistants retrieve that recorded context while Screenpipe is running. Configured cloud AI, transcription, sync, integrations, and connected AI clients can transmit context off-device. Screenpipe is source-available under the [Screenpipe Commercial License](https://github.com/screenpipe/screenpipe/blob/main/LICENSE.md), with a free tier offering limited searchable history and scheduled workflows; see [current plans](https://screenpipe.com/onboarding) for details.
