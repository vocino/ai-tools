Pixwit is an AI video studio focused on trend and meme effects. Upload a photo and generate short clips with templates such as [Tiptoeing in My Jordans](https://pixwit.ai/tiptoeing-in-my-jordans-meme) and [Dime Dime Baby](https://pixwit.ai/dime-dime-baby-ai). It also covers text/image-to-video for ads, avatars, and short sequences on one desk.
