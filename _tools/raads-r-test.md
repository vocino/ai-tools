RAADS-R Test is a free browser-based educational resource for adults who want to organize their answers to an 80-question autism-traits self-assessment, normally completed in about 15–20 minutes. It displays an immediate total score and four-area breakdown, making it easier to revisit patterns rather than treat a single number as a conclusion. The basic questionnaire does not require an account, email address, or payment, and it is not a medical diagnosis, treatment, or claim of AI diagnostic accuracy. Visit https://raads-rtest.online/ for the questionnaire, use the result as a starting point for informed questions rather than a clinical label, and seek qualified professional assessment when appropriate.
