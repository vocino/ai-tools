Video to Video AI transforms a short source clip using prompts that describe a new visual style, setting, lighting, or character direction. The source provides a guide for movement and framing, but the output is a variation rather than a frame-perfect copy. Users can review private 720p or 1080p results, compare them with the source, and download an MP4. It is a browser workflow for video concepts and content creation, not a self-hosted package or a documented public API.
