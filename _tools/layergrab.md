LayerGrab is an AI-assisted image-to-layers workflow for designers and marketers. Paste or upload a flat image, get named editable layers, and export transparent PNG, ZIP, or PSD — not background removal alone, but a full layer-split pipeline for posters, ecommerce creatives, social graphics, and ads. Freemium pricing at https://layergrab.com/pricing.
