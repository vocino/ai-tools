Photoshoot.app is a visual discovery and AI creation platform for ecommerce sellers, creators, and brand teams. Instead of starting from a blank prompt, users explore photoshoot ideas and reference images, upload real products or models, and generate original commercial visuals for product pages, ads, social posts, and lookbooks. Dedicated Product Shoot and OOTD workflows focus on commercial product scenes and outfit photography, with a broader image workspace for general generation and editing.
