VideoTextRemover is an online AI video text remover for creators, YouTubers, marketers and editors. Upload MP4 or MOV (up to 500 MB / 10 min), choose Select area, Subtitles or All text, pick the part to process, and download a clean MP4 up to 1080p. AI inpainting rebuilds pixels under burned-in words instead of blurring or cropping. Freemium: 30 free seconds every month after sign-in; then pay per second. Files deleted after 7 days.
