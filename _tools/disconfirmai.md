DisconfirmAI is a research and reading workspace for individual investors. It pulls daily reading from public sources, digs into earnings reports and company data, and lets you set Radar conditions that flag when something changes. You can write down your own investment framework and check new ideas against it. The research flow puts the evidence against an idea next to the case for it, so you see what could prove you wrong before you commit. There is also an AI 100 list and a set of industry maps. A Chinese version is at https://disconfirmai.com/zh.
