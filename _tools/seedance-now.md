Seedance Now is a browser-based AI video generator for creators who want to try several video models without juggling separate accounts. Write a prompt or upload a still image, choose Seedance 2.5, Kling 4.0 or Wan 3.0, and export clips up to 30 seconds at 1080p without a watermark. New accounts get free credits. It is an independent product and is not affiliated with ByteDance.
