Pulse is an AI-moderated market research platform. An AI moderator runs customer interviews by voice, video, text chat, phone call or WhatsApp, so product, marketing and research teams can collect and review interview results without scheduling every call.

Sign-up is open at app.pulsecorp.co with a free trial, followed by paid plans. Pulse launched publicly in October 2026.
