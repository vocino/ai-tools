Meetini is a meeting and interview assistant for Apple Silicon Macs. Live captions
and transcripts run locally on the Mac; Career AI assistance uses cloud services.
A web companion provides access to meeting history and notes.

Paid plans are described on the [official pricing page](https://meetini.net/pricing).
Notes is $20/month or $45/quarter. Career is $95/month or $240/quarter and includes
600 AI minutes per month. Public launch is planned for November 9, 2026.
